African Energy Bank (AEB) — Financing Africa's Energy Future

Established by the African Petroleum Producers' Organization (APPO) and the African Export-Import Bank (Afreximbank) with $5 Billion initial capital to finance oil, gas, and clean energy infrastructure across APPO member states.

Our Dual Mandate

Balancing critical hydrocarbon development and energy security with a just, sovereign transition to renewable power, solar, wind, and cross-border grid interconnections.

Founding Institutions

Promoted by APPO and Afreximbank, headquartered in Abuja, Federal Republic of Nigeria.

APPO Member States

Algeria, Angola, Benin, Cameroon, Chad, Congo, DR Congo, Côte d'Ivoire, Egypt, Equatorial Guinea, Gabon, Ghana, Libya, Namibia, Niger, Nigeria, Senegal, South Africa.
